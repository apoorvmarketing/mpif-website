About Us

Our Story

MPIF Gurukul was born from a shared belief among a group of leading industrial and technology companies: that talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. Coming together under the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Foundation as a collective CSR initiative in Indore, these companies set out to build more than careers — they set out to build confidence where there is doubt and create opportunity where none exists.

Our 4 Foundational Pillars

01

Ignite Dreams

Every transformation begins with aspiration. We awaken the belief that a better future is possible.

02

Build Confidence

Confidence precedes competence. We build self-belief before technical excellence — inside and out.

03

Provide Exposure

Exposure to industry and institutions broadens perspectives, raises aspirations and opens new doors.

04

Create Role Models

Every successful student becomes a beacon of inspiration for countless others in their community.

Our Vision

To build a generation of confident, skilled, ethical and socially responsible professionals and leaders who pursue excellence, become role models and — sustained by lifelong mentorship — transform their own lives and the lives of their families and communities.

Our Mission

To identify talented, committed and deserving youth from economically disadvantaged backgrounds who have the intent to learn, and empower them through education, mentorship, industry exposure, character building and employment — enabling them to become lifelong learners and catalysts for social change.

Beyond Employability

Our success is not measured by placement numbers alone. The true measure is the long-term transformation of the student, their family and their community — the shift from dependence to dignity and from limited horizons to lasting confidence. Employment is an important milestone on this journey, not its final destination.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MPIF Gurukul, our free 8-month Business Excellence Programme (BXP) in Indore, eligibility, the selection process and placement assistance.

What is MPIF Gurukul?

MPIF Gurukul is a CSR skill training foundation in Indore, an initiative of the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Foundation, that empowers deserving youth through education, mentorship, industry exposure, character development and meaningful employment.

Who can join MPIF Gurukul in Indore?

Graduates and undergraduates are eligible to apply. Selection is based on need, desire to learn, commitment, discipline, aptitude and character, not financial hardship alone.

What training program does MPIF Gurukul offer?

MPIF Gurukul offers a free 6-8 month, 32-week, 640-hour Business Excellence Programme (BXP). It covers professional business skills, AI tools & automation, digital marketing, data management, business case studies, advanced Excel & MIS, with industry visits, corporate exposure and placement preparation.

How long is the MPIF Gurukul course and what are the training hours?

The program runs for 6 months across 24 weeks, with 4 hours per day, 5 days per week, for a total of 480 training hours. Every day follows a proven cycle of Concept, Trainer Demonstration, Student Practice and Activity with Feedback.

Is the MPIF Gurukul training free, and is there placement support?

MPIF Gurukul is a CSR initiative focused on empowering deserving youth, and it aspires to 100 percent meaningful placement for every student because employment is a critical need for the families it serves.

What is the MPIF Gurukul selection and admission process?

Selection follows a 7-step process: application collection, a written entrance test of 60 minutes and 100 marks with a 60 percent cut off, shortlisting, a face-to-face panel interview, final evaluation combining written 60 percent and interview 40 percent with a 65 percent minimum, selection communication, and training enrollment.

What does the entrance test cover?

The written entrance test assesses trainability, basic maths, logic, English and sales aptitude. It carries 100 marks with a recommended minimum cut off of 60 percent to be shortlisted for the interview.

How is the final selection score calculated?

The final score uses a weightage model of written test 60 percent plus interview 40 percent. A minimum final score of 65 percent is recommended, leading to a decision of Selected, Waitlist or Not Selected.

What skills will I learn at MPIF Gurukul?

You will build professional communication, a confident and job-ready personality, and inside and field sales skills, along with corporate work culture readiness. Month 6 focuses on resume building, interview preparation, mock interviews and corporate readiness.

Where is MPIF Gurukul located?

MPIF Gurukul is located near Ahilya Aashram School, BSNL Office, Pologround, Indore, Madhya Pradesh. You can call +91 8889551164 to learn more.

How do I apply to MPIF Gurukul?

Click Apply Now or register via the application form using a Google Form or offline form with your education and contact details. Applications are collected on an ongoing basis.
